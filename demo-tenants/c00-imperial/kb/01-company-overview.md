# Imperial Climate Control — Company Overview

**Imperial Climate Control** designs, installs and maintains climate, ventilation
and life-support systems for large-scale industrial and orbital facilities.
Founded 19 BBY. Headquartered on the Outer Rim, with crews operating across
fourteen sites.

We are a trade contractor. We are not a design consultancy, we do not sell
equipment retail, and we do not work residential.

## What we actually do

- **Life support and atmospheric processing** — the systems that keep a
  facility breathable. This is the majority of our contracted revenue.
- **Thermal regulation** — heating, cooling, and coolant loops for
  high-output installations.
- **Air handling and filtration** — including quarterly filter contracts
  across multi-site accounts.
- **Emergency response** — 24-hour callout for atmospheric, thermal and
  coolant failures under Platinum and Gold plans.

## Who we work for

Facilities managers, station engineers, and construction leads on installations
above 50,000 personnel. Our accounts are ongoing service relationships, not
one-off jobs — most have been with us more than five years.

## Size

Six field technicians, two crews, one dispatcher. Roughly 40 active service
agreements. Annual revenue in the mid eight figures (credits).

## What we are known for

Showing up. Our Platinum response window is four hours and we have met it
99.2% of the time over the last three standard years, including during the
Battle of Yavin.

## What we are not

We are not the manufacturer. When a customer asks us to certify that a
structure's exhaust port design is within specification, that is an engineering
question for the original contractor — not something Imperial Climate Control
can sign off on. We have declined to do so on four occasions and the position
has not changed.
