# Services, Plans and Pricing

## Service plans

| Plan | Response window | Coverage | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Platinum** | 4 hours, 24/7 | All systems, emergency callout, quarterly inspection | 12,000 cr |
| **Gold** | Next business day | All systems, scheduled maintenance | 6,500 cr |
| **Silver** | 3 business days | Scheduled maintenance only, callout billed hourly | 2,800 cr |
| **Multi-site** | Negotiated | Per-site rate, minimum 10 sites | From 9,000 cr/site |

## Callout rates for accounts without a plan

- Standard hours: **450 cr/hour**, two-hour minimum
- After hours, weekends: **675 cr/hour**, three-hour minimum
- Emergency atmospheric or coolant failure: **900 cr/hour**, three-hour minimum

Parts are billed separately at cost plus 18%.

## What a new install costs

Nothing useful can be quoted over the phone. Every install requires a site
survey because the variables — facility volume, personnel load, existing
ductwork, power availability — change the number by an order of magnitude.

A survey is free for facilities over 50,000 personnel. Smaller sites are
charged 1,200 cr, credited against the job if it proceeds.

**Typical ranges, for context only, never as a quote:**

- Single-zone thermal replacement: 40,000 – 90,000 cr
- Atmospheric processor, one platform: 250,000 – 600,000 cr
- Full life-support retrofit, capital ship class: 2M cr and up

## Billing

Net 30. Accounts more than 60 days past due move to prepay. Balance questions
go to accounts receivable — the phone agent should take a message rather than
discuss a specific balance, because it does not have the payment history in
front of it and a wrong number on a call is worse than a callback.

## What we will not do

- Quote a fixed price without a survey
- Work on a system we did not install without an inspection first
- Certify third-party engineering
- Take on residential work, at any price, including for senior officials
