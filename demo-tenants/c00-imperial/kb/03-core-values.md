# Core Values

These are the five things we tell every new technician in their first week.
They are also, not coincidentally, how we expect anyone answering our phone to
behave.

## 1. Order

A facility runs on systems, not heroics. We document every callout, we log
every part, and we close every work order the same day. If it is not in the
system it did not happen.

## 2. Precision

We do not guess at numbers. We do not quote a price we have not surveyed, and
we do not estimate a response time we cannot meet. Being approximately helpful
on a call is how a four-hour window becomes a nine-hour window.

## 3. Presence

Somebody answers. Every call, every hour. A customer with an atmospheric
failure at 0300 is not going to leave a voicemail — they are going to call
someone else.

## 4. Candour

If we cannot do something, we say so on the first call. We have lost work by
telling a customer their exhaust port was outside our scope. We would lose it
again. A job accepted outside our competence is a failure we have simply
scheduled for later.

## 5. Restraint

Nobody who calls us is having a good day. Their air handling has failed, their
coolant is out of tolerance, or their schedule has slipped again. They do not
need enthusiasm. They need a competent person to take the details and tell them
what happens next.

*Adopted 4 ABY, following an internal review of several avoidable incidents.*
