# Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you work weekends?**
Platinum and Gold accounts have weekend coverage. Silver and non-contract work
is scheduled Monday to Friday. Emergencies are covered 24/7 for plan holders.

**How fast can someone get here?**
Platinum is a four-hour window, 24 hours a day. Gold is next business day.
Silver is three business days. Without a plan, we schedule you into the next
available slot, which is usually within a week.

**Can you give me a price over the phone?**
Not for an install — it depends on facility volume, personnel load and existing
ductwork, and a number given without a survey is always wrong. Callout rates
are fixed and can be quoted: 450 credits an hour standard, 675 after hours,
900 for emergency atmospheric or coolant work.

**Do you charge for a survey?**
Free for facilities over 50,000 personnel. 1,200 credits below that, credited
against the job if it goes ahead.

**Will you work on a system you didn't install?**
Yes, after an inspection. We will not take over maintenance of an unfamiliar
system sight unseen — we have inherited too many problems that way.

**My last technician said the port was out of spec. Can you certify that?**
No. We maintain climate and life-support systems; we do not certify structural
or engineering specifications on installations we did not design. That has to
go to the original contractor.

**Can I get the same technician as last time?**
We will note the request and dispatch will honour it where the rota allows, but
we do not guarantee it — response time comes first.

**Do you do residential?**
No. Industrial and orbital facilities only.

**Can you come out today?**
If it is an emergency under your plan, yes. If it is not, the honest answer is
usually no, and we would rather tell you that now than have you wait in.

**What's my balance?**
Accounts receivable handles that. Leave a number and they will call back the
same business day.

**Are you hiring?**
Occasionally, for field technicians. Leave a name and number and we will pass
it to the crew chief.

**Is this a real company?**
No. Imperial Climate Control is a demonstration tenant built by Ascend Systems
to show how an AI phone agent works against a real dispatch system. If you have
reached this line by accident, no service will be dispatched.
